    Our Domed Stickers are supplied in sheet format, not on a roll or individually cut.

    Domed Stickers (also called Domed Labels, Domed Badges, 3D Stickers, 3D Resin Badges, Gel Stickers or Epoxy Stickers) are a popular form of product labeling and brand identity. The polyurethane resin dome provides a hard-wearing, scratch-resistant coating that keeps your logo clear and professional, even in harsh conditions.

    Choose from Gloss White, Brushed Silver, or Brushed Gold vinyl to match your style. With high-resolution CMYK printing and Pantone® matching, your logo stays true to your brand. Use them for electronics, appliances, promotional items and product packaging, or hand them out as swag at trade shows.

    Sizes run from 0.5 inches to nearly 9 inches wide, so they fit almost any surface.

    • Electronics and appliances: a branded nameplate that won't peel or fade.
    • Promotional products: custom logo stickers with a high-end finish.
    • Manufacturing: durable enough for machinery or equipment tags.
    • Event marketing: a premium look at trade shows and expos.

    Printing

    High resolution 4 color (CMYK) digital printing for logos, text and background colors.

    On metallic vinyls, white areas of your design print clear so the metal shows through.

    Pantone® colors

    Pantone® colors are matched with CMYK inks. Some can't be matched exactly and will be converted to the closest CMYK color. Metallic vinyl may affect the final color.

    Custom shapes

    We may adjust designs that can't be domed well, such as sharp points or tight inside curves. Corners need a radius of about 1 mm. Web orders are approved as part of the ordering process.

    How are the domed stickers supplied?

    On easy-peel sheets 9.25 in wide by 10 in tall, with a printable area of 8.81 x 8.64 in. They're not available on a roll or individually cut.

    Do you offer metallic backgrounds?

    Yes. Pick Silver or Gold under Finish. Gloss White is also available.

    Is the doming resin scratch-proof?

    All domed stickers use a scratch-resistant epoxy resin that also gives them a high quality look.

    How thick is the dome?

    Usually around 0.06 in. Production tolerances can vary it by +/- 0.03 in.

    What sizes can I order?

    From 0.5 x 0.5 in up to 8.81 x 8.64 in. Corners need a radius of at least 0.04 in.

    Can AI design my sticker?

    Yes. Describe your sticker to Genie in your own words, or upload your logo. Genie draws logo options or designs layouts to start from, and you can keep asking it for changes, like "make it navy and gold" or "curve the name". Everything stays editable.
